## Patterns
- Adapter: https://refactoring.guru/design-patterns/adapter
- Strategy: https://refactoring.guru/design-patterns/strategy

- Dependency Injection: https://martinfowler.com/articles/injection.html

Patterns are vocabulary, not a reason to create more classes. Ask what vendor behavior is adapted, what decision Strategy owns, whether a new provider can be added without editing every page, and whether Factory/Observer actually solve a problem.

## Research order
1. Reproduce and understand the problem.
2. Read repository architecture and ADRs.
3. Check official documentation.
4. Inspect source/issues when appropriate.
5. Use high-quality secondary material.
6. Treat AI answers as hypotheses to validate, not authority.
